## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Contaminated Ward: Zombie Apocalypse Survival - A survival adventure set inside a contaminated hospital that has been overrun by zombies.

**Storyline:**

There is a huge zombie outbreak that takes over a hospital after some scientists play around with some cures and the experiment goes terribly wrong. The player will begin at the hospital elevator and must explore and gather all seven survival items: A First Aid Kit, a Flashlight, Bottled Water, a Key Card, a gun, a Protective Mask, and the Cure Medicine. The player needs to collect all of the items in order to fight the Mega Zombie and give him the cure in the Research Lab. The player will lose the game if they cannot gather all seven items before facing the Mega Zombie. 

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Elevator - Start room
2. ER Room
3. Triage
4. Trauma Bay
5. Security Office
6. Operating Room
7. Hot Lab
8. Microbiology Lab
9. Research Lab - Villain room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. First Aid Kit
2. Flashlight
3. Bottled Water
4. Key Card
5. Gun
6. Protective Mask
7. The Cure Medicine

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Mega Zombie: The main scientist who turned into the Mega Zombie after the experiment went wrong. The Mega Zombie is located in the Research Lab where the player will fight and stick him with the cure to turn him back to normal. The player will need to gather all seven items before trying to fight the Mega Zombie.
